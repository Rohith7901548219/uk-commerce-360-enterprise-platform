Strongest evidence: auditable source-to-fact reconciliation, actual relational/dbt
execution, clear customer/revenue definitions and temporal ML evaluation. The project
is credible as an independent portfolio when its operational gaps are disclosed.

Prioritise a functioning Docker engine and actual replay/consumer/DLQ demonstration,
then Power BI Desktop assembly, restore tests and model threshold/calibration analysis.
Do not compensate for these gaps with invented financial impact or accuracy claims.

The candidate should answer why AOV uses invoice grain, how duplicate fingerprints can
exclude legitimate identical lines, why missing IDs stay in revenue, how right-censoring
affects labels, why high precision can coexist with low recall, when seasonal-naive
beats a complex forecast, and what happens during a database-commit/offset-commit crash.
The interview question and demo pack expands these into practical exercises.
